# ETERNAL KINGDOM assets

Brand assets are supplied by the project owner. The visual direction uses ivory marble, gold and sky blue.

The three-dimensional architecture, lighting and characters are implemented in `src/three/`. World images and character atlases in `public/world/` support the alternate two-dimensional renderer. Assets are packaged locally rather than hotlinked.

The ambient instrumental sound is synthesized by `src/Ambient.ts`. It starts through an explicit sound control and can be muted. Narration audio is supplied separately with the appropriate publication rights; private faith text is never submitted to an audio provider.

Personal photographs are transient inputs to the appearance tool, not distributed project assets. Dependency licenses are supplied by their respective packages.
